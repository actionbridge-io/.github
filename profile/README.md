# 👋 Welcome to AB (ActionBridge)

**AB (ActionBridge)** is a modern work platform built to help teams move faster with clarity.

We focus on turning everyday work into structured, actionable flows — combining **task management, document management, and sales (CRM)** into a single, seamless experience.

---

## 🚀 What We Build

AB is more than just a task tool.

We design systems that help teams:
- Manage tasks with clear ownership and context  
- Organize documents alongside the work they belong to  
- Track sales and customer relationships (CRM)  
- Reduce noise from scattered chats and emails  
- Turn conversations into real, trackable actions  

Our goal is simple:  
**help teams get the right things done, faster and with better quality.**

---

## 🧠 Our Approach

We believe modern work should be:
- **Context-driven** — everything tied to tasks and outcomes  
- **Collaborative by default** — built where teams already communicate  
- **Structured, not chaotic** — no more lost conversations  
- **AI-assisted** — helping, not replacing, human decisions  

AB integrates deeply with platforms like Microsoft Teams to bring work directly into the flow of communication.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

We build with a pragmatic and scalable stack:

- **Backend:** C# (.NET / ASP.NET Core)  
- **AI / Data / Automation:** Python  
- **Cloud:** Microsoft Azure  
- **Integration:** Microsoft Graph / Teams Platform
